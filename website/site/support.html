Support

Ask the right operator.

For sign-in, account, playlist, media, report, or deletion problems, contact the administrator of the selected server first. The Ichoi developer cannot access an independently operated server.

Project support contact: support@ichoi.invalid. This is a placeholder until approved.

Include useful details

  • The selected server URL.
  • The client and version.
  • The action that failed and the time it failed.
  • A short error message. Do not send your password or session token.

For account removal, follow account deletion help. For privacy questions, read the privacy policy.
