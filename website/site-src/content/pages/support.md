<p class="eyebrow">Support</p>
<h1>Ask the right operator.</h1>
<p>For sign-in, account, playlist, media, report, or deletion problems, contact the administrator of the selected server first. The Ichoi developer cannot access an independently operated server.</p>
<p>Project support contact: <a href="mailto:support@ichoi.invalid">support@ichoi.invalid</a>. This is a placeholder until approved.</p>
<h2>Include useful details</h2><ul><li>The selected server URL.</li><li>The client and version.</li><li>The action that failed and the time it failed.</li><li>A short error message. Do not send your password or session token.</li></ul>
<p>For account removal, follow <a href="account-deletion.html">account deletion help</a>. For privacy questions, read the <a href="privacy.html">privacy policy</a>.</p>
