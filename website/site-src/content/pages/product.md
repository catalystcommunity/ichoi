<p class="eyebrow">Product</p>
<h1>Listen from a server you choose.</h1>
<p class="lead">Ichoi connects to a compatible server. It lets you browse available music, play it, and manage your account on that server.</p>
<h2>What Ichoi provides</h2>
<ul><li>Native Android and iOS clients.</li><li>Server profiles for servers that you choose.</li><li>Account sign-in and secure session storage.</li><li>Browsing and streaming from the selected server.</li><li>Playlist and account reporting to the selected server.</li></ul>
<h2>What Ichoi does not provide</h2>
<p>Ichoi does not include music, host accounts, or supply a default music catalogue. You supply the server and media. A server operator decides which media is available and how long server data stays on the server.</p>
<p>Ichoi is always free. It has no ads, purchases, subscriptions, analytics, remote crash reporting, or donation functions. It is available under the <a href="license.html">Apache-2.0 licence</a>.</p>
