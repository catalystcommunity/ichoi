# Content rating and user content answers

This document is a draft. A person must enter the answers in Play Console and
record the rating that Google assigns.

## Target audience

- Select ages 18 and over only.
- Do not select an age group below 18.
- Do not join the Families program.

## User content

- Users can interact through content on a selected server: Yes.
- Users can share user-created content: Yes. Playlist names, display names, and
  public playlists can be visible to other users of that server.
- Users can report content or users: Yes. A signed-in user can report a playlist
  or an account.
- Report reasons: Objectionable content, harassment, spam, and other.
- A moderator reviews reports: Yes. The selected server operator reviews them.
- A moderator can remove content or accounts: Yes. A server administrator can
  delete a playlist or an account, except for the last administrator.
- Users can block another user: No. Ichoi has no direct messaging or one-to-one
  social function.
- Terms are required before content creation: Yes. A user must accept the terms
  before the client creates a playlist.

## Content rating

Answer questions about user interaction and shared user content with "Yes."
Answer advertising, purchases, gambling, and location-sharing questions with
"No." Do not predict the final rating. Record the assigned rating after the
questionnaire is complete.
