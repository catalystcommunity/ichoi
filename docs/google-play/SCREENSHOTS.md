# Google Play screenshot plan

Do not create store screenshots with placeholder URLs or test data. Capture the
screens only after the production demo passes its public smoke test.

Use one supported phone size and the current Play Console image rules. Also
prepare larger layouts only if the final application supports those device
classes.

Capture these real functions:

1. First-run explanation with **Connect to demo server** visible.
2. Music library after the demo connects.
3. Album details with the play control.
4. Now playing and the media notification.
5. Playlist details with the report action.
6. Settings with the privacy and account controls.

Use only approved demo recordings and artwork. Do not show private account data,
development IDs, `.invalid` URLs, unapproved licence claims, or third-party
trademarks. Do not use a frame or caption that implies that music is included in
the application.
