# Reviewer access draft

The production demo is at `https://ichoi.catalystsquad.com`. Guests can browse and play
without a login. Do not submit these instructions until the store-review account exists and
the signed-in steps pass.

## Play Console application access

No login is needed for browsing and playback.

1. Open Ichoi and tap **I accept**.
2. Tap **Connect to demo server**.

The application connects to `https://ichoi.catalystsquad.com` and opens the library.
Select an album or audiobook, then tap its play control.

Reporting, blocking, and account deletion need the dedicated store-review member account.
Add the exact LinkKeys identity and password in the private Play Console access record. Do
not put the credential in this file. Give steps to sign in, report a playlist and its owner,
block and unblock that owner, and delete the reviewer account. A later login recreates the
trusted reviewer account.

## Required replacement
